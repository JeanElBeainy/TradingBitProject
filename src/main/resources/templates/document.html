Project Documentation

1. Introduction

TradingBit.net is a full-stack web application designed as a simulated cryptocurrency trading platform. The platform allows users to sign up, view real-time market data, manage a simulated portfolio, and execute mock trades (swaps).

The core purpose of this project is educational. It is not a real financial service, and no real money is involved. For more details, please refer to the Disclaimer.

2. Core Features

User Authentication

Secure user registration and login system handled by Spring Security. Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt to ensure data protection.

Market Dashboard

Displays a comprehensive table of the top 100 cryptocurrencies with data fetched from the CoinMarketCap API. Information includes price, market cap, 24h volume, and circulating supply. The data on this page auto-refreshes every 60 seconds using htmx.

Crypto Swapping

Users can swap cryptocurrencies from their portfolio. The system calculates exchange rates based on live prices and applies a 0.1% platform fee to simulate real-world trading costs.

Account & Profile Overview

A dedicated profile page where users can view their total portfolio balance, broken down by USD and crypto holdings. It also displays a detailed history of all swap transactions, including pairs, quantities, fees, and timestamps.

3. Getting Started

To use the platform, a user must first create an account:

  1. Navigate to the sign up page.
  2. Fill in your username, email, and a unique password.
  3. Read and accept the terms by checking the box linked to the disclaimer.
  4. Once registered, you can Sign In to access the dashboard.

Upon signing up, your account is credited with a starting balance of $1,000,000 USD in simulated funds to begin trading.

4. Executing a Swap

The Swap page is where all the trading occurs. The process is straightforward:

  1. You select an asset you own (e.g., USD or Bitcoin) in the "From" field.
  2. You select the asset you wish to acquire in the "To" field.
  3. After entering an amount and clicking the "swap" button, the system calculates the transaction. A platform fee of 0.1% is applied to every swap and is deducted from the final amount you receive.
  4. A slippage of 1% is applied to protect the user from getting worse prices during volatility.

5. Profile & Trade History

Every swap you complete is immediately recorded. You can review a complete history of your activity on the Account Overview page. This creates a direct link between your trading actions and your historical records.
Each entry in your history details contains:

  1. The trading pair (e.g., BTC → ETH).
  2. The exact quantities swapped.
  3. The price of each asset at the time of the trade.
  4. The total USD volume of the transaction.
  5. The fee that was paid.
  6. The timestamp of the trade.
